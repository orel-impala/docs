Impala can send a webhook to your endpoint whenever we change the RPM or TPM limit on your team. If you route traffic across several providers, use it to keep Impala's share of traffic within the new limit instead of finding out from `429` responses.

Webhooks are enabled per team on request. Ask your Impala account team to turn them on.

## Set up an endpoint

1. Ask your Impala account team to enable webhooks for your team.
2. In [Settings](https://platform.getimpala.ai/settings), add an HTTPS endpoint that accepts `POST` requests with a JSON body.
3. Ask us to send a test event and check that it arrives.
