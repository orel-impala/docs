The docs explain what Impala does. These explain why it's built that way.

Impala's [Research](https://www.getimpala.ai/research) page covers the engine and its measured results; the writing below is on the [Impala blog](https://www.getimpala.ai/blog).

## Cost and utilization

**[Run It Hot](https://www.getimpala.ai/blog/run-it-hot)** — Why utilization, not electricity price, is the lever on cost per token. Doubling throughput halves cost per million tokens; halving your power price moves it about five percent. The arithmetic behind [Cheaper inference, better quality](/async-inference).
